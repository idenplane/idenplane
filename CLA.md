# Idenplane Contributor License Agreement

Version 1.0

This is the Contributor License Agreement ("CLA") for the Idenplane project. It is adapted from the Apache Software Foundation's [Individual Contributor License Agreement v2.2](https://www.apache.org/licenses/icla.pdf) and [Software Grant and Corporate Contributor License Agreement](https://www.apache.org/licenses/cla-corporate.pdf). The changes from the Apache text are: who receives the license, that it covers past contributions as well, an explicit right to license contributions under other terms, a commitment that contributions stay available under the open-source license, the right to transfer the agreement, and how it is signed.

**Why there is a CLA.** The Idenplane server is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0-only). The maintainer also licenses the server commercially, to organizations that cannot comply with the AGPL, and runs it as a hosted service. A contribution received only under the AGPL could not be included in those. This agreement gives the maintainer that right. You keep the copyright in your contribution, and section 4 commits the maintainer to keep your contribution available under the open-source license of the part of Idenplane you contributed to. [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#contributor-license-agreement) explains when you need to sign and how.

The two paragraphs above are an explanation. The agreement is the text that follows.
